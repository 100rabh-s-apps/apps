🏰 Welcome to Mind Mansion!

Ready to unlock a Memory Superpower? We use the Method of Loci (The Memory Palace) to help you remember anything!

How to Play:

  1. Place: Drag items from the bottom into the numbered circles.
  2. Paint: Choose a "silly modifier" (like making it ENORMOUS) to lock it in your brain.
  3. Play: Once all 10 are hidden, click the circles in order and name the item that was there!

🧠 The Secret Sauce for Clear Memory:

  • Be Vivid: Don't just think of a cat; think of a neon-purple cat wearing a tutu!
  • Use Space: Your brain is amazing at remembering where things are. By putting items in specific spots, you give them a permanent home.
  • Get Weird: Our brains ignore "normal" things. If you make an item dance or smell like stinky socks, your brain says, "Whoa, I need to remember that!"

"The weirder you paint it, the easier you'll find it!"
