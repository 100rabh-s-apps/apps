StoryMaker

created by 100rabh

How to Play: Enter your part of the story in the text area and click 'Add to Story'. The AI will then continue the narrative!

About StoryMaker: This application allows you to collaboratively write stories with an AI. The AI model, Xenova/LaMini-T5-738M, is a large language model (LLM) that runs entirely in your browser, thanks to the Hugging Face Transformers.js library. This means your story data never leaves your device, ensuring privacy and allowing for offline use once the model is loaded.
